Everything on this page is a change to your brain, not to hos's code. Each one is a recipe in the `hos` skill. Ask
your agent: "Use the hos skill to …" and it asks you a few questions, makes the change, checks it, and tells you how
to undo it. Brain recipes end with `hos validate` passing.

This page is built from the recipe files themselves, so it says what your agent will do. Each section's fold shows the
agent's part of the recipe.
